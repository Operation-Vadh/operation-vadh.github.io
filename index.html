OPERATION VADH

The shadows hide more than you think

They operate in darkness

Scammers. Fraudsters. Malicious actors. They thrive in the shadows of the internet, believing no one is watching. They prey on the innocent, steal from the vulnerable, and corrupt our digital world.

But we see them. And we're calling on everyone with the skills and courage to bring them into the light.

If you understand OSINT, cybersecurity, or digital investigation—your knowledge is needed. This January, we hunt those who hide in darkness.

Ab hoga Vadh......

Just like the iconic dialogue from the movie "Vadh", "Humne hatya nahi, vadh kiya hai vadh," our mission is to eliminate digital criminals and bring justice to the online world. Inspired by the story of a common man standing up against wrongdoers, we are committed to making the internet a safer place for everyone.

Your Mission

DIRECTIVE 01

Identify Targets

Use OSINT techniques to discover scam websites, phishing operations, fraudulent platforms, and malicious actors operating in the shadows.

Track their digital footprints. Document URLs, hosting details, domain information, and infrastructure. Build comprehensive profiles.
DIRECTIVE 02

Gather Evidence

Collect irrefutable proof. Screenshots, archives, metadata, network maps. Build cases that cannot be ignored or dismissed.

Use every tool available: WHOIS lookups, reverse searches, archive services, network analysis. Leave no stone unturned.
DIRECTIVE 03

Create Awareness

Expose their operations publicly. Create content that educates and warns others. Share using #OperationVadh to build collective knowledge.

Write threads, create videos, publish reports. Make it impossible for them to operate unnoticed.
DIRECTIVE 04

Report Bad Actors

File official reports with hosting providers, domain registrars, abuse databases, and law enforcement. Turn evidence into action.

Contact abuse departments, submit to cybercrime units, alert platform moderators. Make their existence untenable.
DIRECTIVE 05

Coordinate Efforts

Share intelligence with others. Verify findings. Amplify important discoveries. We are stronger when we work together.

Collaborate on investigations. Support fellow researchers. Build a network of digital defenders.

Join the Operation

Every scammer exposed is progress. Every malicious site taken down is a victory. Every person protected is justice. You have the skills. You have the knowledge. Now use them.
